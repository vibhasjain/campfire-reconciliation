# Maya's close — Campfire

Twelve frames, one illustration language. Every image prompt starts with the STYLE block verbatim, then the SCENE paragraph for that frame.

## STYLE (prefix for every frame — do not vary)

Storyboard panel, 16:9 widescreen, detailed editorial graphic-novel illustration. Confident black ink linework with flat cel shading and a subtle paper grain. Strictly limited palette: deep charcoal (#141416) shadows, warm cream (#efe9dc) paper lights, cool slate mid-tones, and exactly ONE signal colour, Ember lime (#B2EC96), used sparingly on the single most important element in the frame. Cinematic composition with clear foreground / mid-ground / background depth, one dramatic directional light source, realistic adult human proportions, contemporary American finance and accounting teams at mid-size tech companies (open-plan offices, home offices, late-night month-end close), lots of believable environmental detail. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, readable UI copy or watermarks anywhere in the image. Not 3D render, not clay, not chibi, not cartoon mascot, not photoreal.

## Continuity

Maya Patel is an adult Indian American woman with shoulder-length dark hair, a rolled-sleeve cream blouse, and slate trousers. Keep her face, hair, clothes, and ordinary adult proportions consistent. Priya Shah, her AP teammate, is a distinct adult Indian American woman with tied-back curly hair, round glasses, and a charcoal cardigan. Daniel Kim, her controller, is a distinct adult Korean American man with short dark hair and a slate shirt. They work at Arbor Analytics, a mid-size software company; do not draw company or bank branding.

Ember is a translucent wireframe adult figure or pair of hands, never a mascot. Render Ember in cool slate whenever another object carries the single lime accent. Recurring objects are the two long paper tapes for bank and ledger, compared on a lightbox; a small balance scale; and removable paperclips. Tapes and evidence use blank blocks, perforations, and non-linguistic marks, never letters, digits, readable handwriting, or financial symbols. The captions hold the account details and amounts; the art holds the feeling. Frame 01 establishes ordinary close pressure, not despair. Frames 02 onward carry pleasure, momentum, and Maya's judgment. The same window, desk lamp, mug, and shallow trays recur through the office scenes. Compose for full-bleed 16:9 cropping, keeping the focal object and faces clear of the outer edges.
